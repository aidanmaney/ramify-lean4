# thumbs.mjs for the pictures). Each card's title comes from its file's
# leading `/-! # … -/` block; a `## Heading` line starts a group.
# `<file> <proof>` names the proof the card opens and pictures (else the
# file's longest); ` = Title` after it is the card's title where the file's
# own is long; `@hero <name>` is the picture the index opens with.
